Mode: Operate. Scope: public/index.html, public/app.js, public/style.css.
The user delegated implementation and design decisions. Code-first execution.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A transfer desk where selection and evidence sit side by side. The conversation ledger is the main surface; status follows the actual verification result.

OWN-WORLD: Warm white paper, dark green ink, muted green action, amber for uncertain outcomes. DM Sans, compact tabular dates, 1px rules, squared table rows, generous outer margins. No illustrations or promotional metrics.

STORY: Connect two distinct accounts, browse and preview, assemble a selection, move it, inspect the receipts. A count always describes real selected or verified conversations.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Small wordmark and language control. Title and short purpose. Source-to-destination account strip. Two-thirds conversation ledger with filters, one-third selection and migration controls. The primary action stays beside the selected count. Mobile stacks these regions without clipping rows.

FORM: Candidate 5, transfer dispatch ledger. Other grounded candidates: mail archive, file browser, compare editor, checklist workbook, desk ledger, conversation cards, task timeline. Seed bb8436d0. Catalog challengers were declined for weaker task clarity; keep their disciplined alignment, typography, explicit state marks and reserved accent use. Signature interaction: selection survives filtering; a selected row's mark and the count update together, while preview opens in the adjacent desk.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
